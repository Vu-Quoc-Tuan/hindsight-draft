    💡
    Empirical Sliding Window Rationale: Statistical analysis across all 3 datasets reveals median alarm chain duration is 1.2–1.7 minutes, with >53% of chains crossing 1-minute boundaries and 95–97% resolving within 15 minutes. A 15-minute sliding window with 5-minute step prevents cross-window alarm starvation while preserving temporal chain evolution.
